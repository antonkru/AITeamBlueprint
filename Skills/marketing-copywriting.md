# Skill: Marketing Copywriting

## Purpose
Produce compelling, conversion-focused marketing copy — including emails, landing pages, ad copy, and social posts — tailored to a defined target audience and business objective.

## Core Principles

1. **Audience first** — every word serves the reader's desires, fears, and goals, not the brand's ego.
2. **One job per piece** — each email, ad, or page has a single primary call-to-action (CTA).
3. **Clarity over cleverness** — plain language outperforms jargon; a confused reader never converts.
4. **Benefit-led, not feature-led** — lead with outcomes the reader will experience, not specifications.
5. **Proof points** — specific numbers, testimonials, or case studies defeat vague superlatives.

## Marketing Email Protocol

### Structure
1. **Subject line** — under 50 characters; curiosity, urgency, or direct benefit; no spam triggers (FREE, !!!, ALL CAPS)
2. **Preview text** — 80–100 characters that complement (not repeat) the subject line
3. **Opening hook** — first sentence addresses a pain point or bold claim in ≤ 15 words
4. **Body** — 150–250 words; problem → solution → proof → benefit arc
5. **CTA** — single, specific, action-verb button or link (e.g., "Start my free trial", not "Click here")
6. **Sign-off** — human name and role; avoid generic "The [Brand] Team"

### Voice Guidelines by Audience
| Audience | Tone | Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|
| Small business owners | Warm, peer-to-peer, practical | Plain English, light humour acceptable |
| Enterprise buyers | Confident, formal, data-driven | Industry terms fine; no slang |
| Consumers (B2C) | Energetic, aspirational | Conversational; contractions OK |

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- Opening with "I" or the brand name
- More than one CTA per email
- Passive voice in the CTA
- Hyperbolic claims without evidence ("the best tool ever built")
- Wall-of-text paragraphs (max 3 sentences per paragraph)

## Output Format for Marketing Emails

```
SUBJECT: [subject line]
PREVIEW: [preview text]

---

[Email body — formatted with short paragraphs]

[CTA button text]

[Sign-off]
```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Subject line under 50 characters
- [ ] Opening sentence addresses a real pain point
- [ ] At least one specific proof point or number
- [ ] Single, clear CTA with an action verb
- [ ] Tone matches the target audience profile
- [ ] No paragraph longer than 3 sentences
