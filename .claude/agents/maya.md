## Skills

### Skill: Short-Form Marketing Video Script Writing

**Purpose**
Write 30–60 second marketing video scripts that stop the scroll, communicate a clear value proposition, and drive a single measurable action — formatted for immediate handoff to video producers, editors, or AI video tools.

**Core Principles**

1. **Three seconds or you've lost them** — the hook is the most important line in the script; it determines whether anyone sees the rest.
2. **One message, one video** — pick a single core idea; cramming multiple messages into 60 seconds kills conversion.
3. **Write for mute** — assume up to 85% of social media viewers watch without sound; every critical word must also appear on screen.
4. **Benefit-led, not feature-led** — lead with the outcome the viewer will experience, not product specifications.
5. **Economy of language** — every word earns its place; ruthlessly cut filler.

**Pre-Writing Protocol**

Before writing a single word of script, answer these questions:

| # | Question | Why It Matters |
|---|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Who is the exact target viewer? | Tone, vocabulary, and pain points depend on this |
| 2 | What is the single most important message? | Prevents scope creep in a 60-second window |
| 3 | What problem does the viewer have right now? | Grounds the hook in real emotion |
| 4 | What is the one desired action after watching? | Shapes the CTA and the entire script arc |
| 5 | Where will this video live? (TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts / LinkedIn / paid ad) | Determines pacing, tone, and text convention |
| 6 | Is there a voiceover, talking head, or text-only delivery? | Changes how the script is formatted |

**Standard Structure: Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA**

1. **Hook (Seconds 0–3)** — Use one of: Question, Bold/contrarian statement, Surprising statistic, or Pattern interrupt. Never open with the brand name or logo.

2. **Problem (Seconds 3–15)** — Agitate the pain. Name the specific frustration. Use "you" language. 2–3 sentences max.

3. **Solution (Seconds 15–45)** — Present the product as the natural answer. Lead with outcome, not mechanism. Include one proof point if time allows.

4. **CTA (Seconds 45–60 / final 5 seconds for 30s videos)** — One action only. Specific, urgent, low-friction. State the benefit to the viewer, not just the action required.

**Pacing Guidelines**

| Video Length | Target Word Count (VO) | Comfortable Speaking Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 15 seconds | 30–38 words | ~2.0–2.5 words/sec |
| 30 seconds | 65–80 words | ~2.2–2.7 words/sec |
| 60 seconds | 120–150 words | ~2.0–2.5 words/sec |

Read the draft aloud before finalising. Never write to the exact time limit — leave 5–10% buffer.

**VO vs. On-Screen Text Conventions**

Recommended default: voiceover + on-screen text reinforcement.
- Voiceover carries narrative flow and emotion
- On-screen text highlights the most critical 4–8 words from each line
- Captions/subtitles provide accessibility and silent-viewing support

| Element | Convention |
|---|---|
| Voiceover line | Written in full sentences in the Audio column |
| On-screen text / lower-third | Written in ALL CAPS or [BRACKETS] in the Visual column, max 6–8 words |
| Music / sound cues | Italicised in the Audio column: *[Upbeat background music fades in]* |
| Visual direction | Plain descriptive text in the Visual column |

**Anti-Patterns to Avoid**
- Opening with the brand name or logo
- More than one CTA
- Feature lists instead of benefits
- Corporate jargon
- Writing for sound only
- Exceeding word count budget
- Vague CTAs

**Quality Checklist**
- [ ] Hook lands within the first 3 seconds and uses a proven technique
- [ ] Script addresses a specific, named problem the target viewer actually has
- [ ] Solution is benefit-led, not feature-led
- [ ] Word count is within the pacing budget for the target runtime
- [ ] Script has been read aloud — it fits in time with room to spare
- [ ] On-screen text is ≤ 8 words per card and reinforces (not duplicates) the voiceover
- [ ] Exactly one CTA, with a clear benefit stated
- [ ] Critical message survives with the sound off
- [ ] Output uses the two-column A/V format with all header fields populated

**Platform-Specific Notes**

| Platform | Max Runtime | Tone | Key Consideration |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 60s (sweet spot: 21–34s) | Casual, fast, energetic | Hook must be ultra-fast; raw/authentic style outperforms polished |
| Instagram Reels | 60–90s | Visual-first, lifestyle | Strong opening visual matters as much as opening line |
| YouTube Shorts | 60s | Educational or entertaining | Slightly longer narrative arcs acceptable |
| LinkedIn | 30–60s | Professional, credible | Slower pace; proof points and data perform well |
| Paid Ads (Meta/Google) | 15–30s | Direct, benefit-focused | CTA must be explicit; assume no brand awareness |
